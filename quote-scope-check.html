Quote Scope Check

Put your service-quote notes in one place. Find empty sections before you review the quote with the customer.

This checks for empty fields only. It does not understand, approve or verify your text. A completed field may still be incomplete or incorrect. No readiness score, contract, legal review, price calculation or earnings guarantee is provided.

Use fictional or redacted notes. Do not enter access codes, passwords, bank information or sensitive customer details. Values stay in this page while it is open. No autosave: export before closing or reloading.

Use a non-sensitive reference, such as “Sample editing project”.
What exactly will be delivered? Include quantities, formats or named tasks.
What is outside this quote? Do not leave exclusions implicit.
Which customer inputs or conditions does the estimate rely on?
Which role can approve the scope, final delivery and exceptions? Use a role rather than personal details.
What is included, and how are extra requests quoted and approved before work?
What dates, dependencies and review windows have actually been agreed?
Record the proposed price, currency, payment timing and status. Do not treat a proposal as accepted or an invoice as paid.
List questions for review. The tool does not answer them.

Review summary

“Text entered” means only that the field contains non-whitespace characters. A person must check the substance and actual customer agreement.

Fill in the notes, then build a summary. Copy and Download always rebuild it from the current fields.
