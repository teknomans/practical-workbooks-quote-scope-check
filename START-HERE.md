# Quote Scope Check — free local tool v0.1

Extract the ZIP and open `quote-scope-check.html` in a current desktop browser. No installation, account, external AI subscription, server or API is required. The two optional Payhip links open external product pages only when you choose them.

Use fictional or redacted notes. Do not enter sensitive customer records, credentials, access codes or financial account information. This file has no network requests, cloud sync, local storage or autosave. Your notes exist in this page until it is closed/reloaded or you clear them. Export before leaving.

1. Enter a non-sensitive reference and notes for deliverables, exclusions, assumptions, approval owner, changes/revisions, timing and price/payment terms.
2. Click Build review summary. Empty or whitespace-only sections are listed as missing. Other sections are labeled “Text entered; not verified.”
3. Review the substance yourself. A one-word or contradictory entry still passes the presence check. The tool does not interpret language or evaluate the adequacy of your quote.
4. Copy TXT or Download TXT. Both rebuild the summary from current values, even if you edited after the last preview. Confirm your browser actually saved the download before closing.
5. Clear all notes removes fields and preview from the page immediately. Previously saved files are not affected. There is no undo/restore feature for this button.

Copy may be blocked by browser permissions or local-file clipboard restrictions. Use Download TXT or select and copy the summary manually. Browser print styles are provided as a convenience, but native printing/PDF and actual phone behavior have not been verified.

## What this does not provide

No pricing calculation, numerical readiness score, contract, legal review, safety assessment, compliance certification, acceptance of customer terms, payment processing, automated message sending or earnings guarantee. No AI inference is performed. Placeholders are examples, not recommended contractual terms. The owner/customer must agree what applies to the real work.

The existing Video Editor Quote Workshop and Cleaning Quote & Actual Profit Toolkit are optional separate paid products. They are not included with this free tool.
