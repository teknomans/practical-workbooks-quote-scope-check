# SOP handover checklist: can someone else follow your process?

Published by Practical Workbooks on November 12, 2026.

A process can make sense to its author and still be hard to hand over. Try this checklist before someone else uses your instructions.

## Copy this handover checklist

1. **Trigger:** What starts the task?
2. **Owner:** Who does the work and reviews the result?
3. **Inputs:** Which files or facts are needed first?
4. **Steps:** What happens, in what order?
5. **Exceptions:** When should the person stop and ask for help?
6. **Evidence:** What shows completion?
7. **Version:** Who last checked the instructions, and when?

## Fictional example: an edit ready for review

Trigger: an editor marks a draft ready. Inputs: agreed format and current file. Steps: check the filename, open the export, check its beginning and end, and compare with agreed deliverables. Stop if it will not play or the format is unclear. Evidence: record the version and review owner.

This is a writing prompt, not a complete technical quality standard.

## Test the handover

Ask a teammate to explain the next step using only your draft. Add any missing context to the instructions. Keep unresolved questions visible. Never put passwords, access codes or private customer records in shared examples.
